Privacy policy

Short version: nothing you type leaves your phone. TsumuKey has no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and collects no data about you.

Last updated: 9 November 2026.

The keyboard

  • It never goes online. Predictions, corrections and Japanese conversion run on your phone.
  • What it keeps, on your phone only: words you taught it (by undoing a correction), your recently used emoji, and its settings. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Clipboard: the keyboard reads the clipboard only when you tap its paste button.

Full Access

iOS shows the same warning for every keyboard with Full Access, saying it could send what you type. TsumuKey uses Full Access only to read the settings and models the TsumuKey app keeps for it, and to paste. It sends nothing.

The app

  • The app goes online when you tap Check for models or download a model. It then fetches the model list and files from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). As with any website, Hugging Face sees your IP address and the files requested; see the Hugging Face privacy policy.
  • On mobile data, the app asks before downloading models.
  • Currency conversion (off until you turn it on): the app then fetches the European Central Bank's daily exchange rates, one public file from ecb.europa.eu, about once a day. The ECB sees your IP address, as with any website. The keyboard reads the saved rates and stays offline. Turning the setting off deletes them.
  • Nothing you typed, and nothing about you, is sent with these requests.

Changes

If a future feature needs the network, such as GIF search, it will be off until you turn it on, and this page will say what is sent and to whom before it ships.
